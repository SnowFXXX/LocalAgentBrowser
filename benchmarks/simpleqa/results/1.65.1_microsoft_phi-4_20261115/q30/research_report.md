# Research report

**Objective:** Which patch for the original Dark Souls reduced the effectiveness of the Hornet Ring?

**Status:** finished by the agent on turn 20

## Final result

The 1.05 patch for the original Dark Souls reduced the effectiveness of the Hornet Ring. The patch notes indicate that under the heading 'Status Changes,' the base equipment burden was increased by approximately 6-10 points. This change likely affected the Hornet Ring, which is known to reduce equipment load, thereby reducing its overall effectiveness. The specific patch note supporting this is [1].
