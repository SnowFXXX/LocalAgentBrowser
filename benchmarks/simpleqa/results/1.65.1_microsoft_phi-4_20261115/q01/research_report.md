# Research report

**Objective:** Who won the Gerard P. Kuiper Prize in 2001?

**Status:** finished by the agent on turn 4

## Final result

The winner of the Gerard P. Kuiper Prize in 2001 was Bruce W. Hapke, Professor of Planetary Sciences in the Department of Geology and Planetary Science of the University of Pittsburgh. He was chosen by the Division for Planetary Sciences of the American Astronomical Society as the winner of the Kuiper Prize for the year 2001. [1]
