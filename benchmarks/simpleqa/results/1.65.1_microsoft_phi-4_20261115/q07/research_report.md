# Research report

**Objective:** What two football teams were mentioned in the libel case against "The Saturday Evening Post" in 1963?

**Status:** finished by the agent on turn 4

## Final result

The two football teams mentioned in the libel case against 'The Saturday Evening Post' in 1963 were the Georgia Bulldogs and Alabama. The lawsuit was filed by Wally Butts, the former Georgia Bulldogs football coach, against the magazine, which alleged that Butts and the Alabama head coach Bear Bryant had conspired to fix games.
