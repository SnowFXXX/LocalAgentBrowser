# Research report

**Objective:** What is the name of Nobita Nobi's paternal grandfather?

**Status:** finished by the agent on turn 4

## Final result

The name of Nobita Nobi's paternal grandfather is Nobiru Nobi. This information is supported by discovery [1], which states that 'Nobiru Nobi is Nobita's paternal grandfather.'

## Discoveries (1)

### 1. Turn 3, from https://doraemon.fandom.com/wiki/Nobiru_Nobi

(Under the heading "Skin color") Nobiru Nobi is Nobita's paternal grandfather, the father-in-law of Tamako Nobi, the husband of Nobita's grandmother, and also the father of Nobirou Nobi and Nobisuke Nobi. When Nobita's father was little, he used to be treated in a disciplined manner by Nobita's grandfather. Nobita's paternal grandfather passed away before Nobita was born (or Nobisuke had married with Tamako). Nobita's paternal grandfather appears in a few episodes. Nobita and Doraemon meet him by going to the past using a time machine. He appears to love his son a lot, but does not seem to express his affection to his son, so that he doesn't get spoiled.
