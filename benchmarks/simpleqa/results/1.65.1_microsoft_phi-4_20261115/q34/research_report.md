# Research report

**Objective:** In September 2022, biologists reported what number of Devils Hole Pupfish in Devils Hole, the most they'd observed in 19 years?

**Status:** finished by the agent on turn 4

## Final result

In September 2022, biologists reported 263 Devils Hole Pupfish in Devils Hole, which is the most they'd observed in 19 years. This count is the highest autumn count recorded since September 2003 [1].
