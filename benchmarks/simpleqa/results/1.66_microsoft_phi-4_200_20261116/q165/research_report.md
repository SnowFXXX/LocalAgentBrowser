# Research report

**Objective:** What were the names of the two streets at the intersection where the Sydenham Hospital in New York was originally located?

**Status:** finished by the agent on turn 4

## Final result

The names of the two streets at the intersection where the Sydenham Hospital in New York was originally located were 124th Street and Manhattan Avenue. This information is supported by discovery [1].
