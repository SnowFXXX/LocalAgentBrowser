## Final result

The play 'Agamemnon' at the 2022 Athens Epidaurus Festival was directed by Ulrich Rasche. He is noted for his unusual and impressive scenic language, and his actors are often driven towards a deeply existential and 'manic' style of performance, combining text, movement, and rhythm. Audiences will be captivated by a breathtaking production, a well-orchestrated choreography performed on mechanical revolving stages to the sounds of live contemporary music. [1]
