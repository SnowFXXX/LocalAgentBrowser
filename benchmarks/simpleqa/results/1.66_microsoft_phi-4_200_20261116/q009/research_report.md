## Final result

The name of the cannibal in the Whistleblower DLC of the 2013 video game 'Outlast' is Frank Antonio Manera. He was a cannibalistic Variant who roamed Mount Massive Asylum's Hospital at the time of the Mount Massive Asylum Incident. [1]
