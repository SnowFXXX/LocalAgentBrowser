# Research report

**Objective:** How many operator cells are within the Yamaha YM2203 from the 1980s?

**Status:** finished by the agent on turn 4

## Final result

The Yamaha YM2203 from the 1980s includes 12 operator 'cells', each generating a 13-bit sine wave at a programmable frequency, with volume controlled by a programmable ADSR envelope generator. This information is supported by discovery [1].
