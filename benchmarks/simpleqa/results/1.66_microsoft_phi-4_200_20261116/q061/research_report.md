# Research report

**Objective:** What is the name of the man who purchased Belmont on St. Saviour's Hill, Jersey, UK, in 1822?

**Status:** finished by the agent on turn 8

## Final result

The name of the man who purchased Belmont on St. Saviour's Hill, Jersey, UK, in 1822 is Major-General Sir Colin Halkett. This information is supported by discoveries [1] and [2], which mention that he was the lieutenant governor who transacted the exchange for the Belmont property.

## Discoveries (2)

### 1. Turn 3, from https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_House,_Jersey

(Under the heading "Previous Government Housesedit") The current building is at least the fifth official residence of governors and lieutenant governors of Jersey. The current Government House was previously known as Belmont. Major-General Sir Colin Halkett was petitioning the British Government in 1821 to agree the exchange of this town centre property.

### 2. Turn 6, from https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_House,_Jersey

(Under the heading "The current Government Houseedit") In 1822, Major-General Sir Colin Halkett, lieutenant governor, transacted the exchange of the old Government House in Saint Helier and Le Pré au Roi in Saint Clement for the Belmont property and additional land.
