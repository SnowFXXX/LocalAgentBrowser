# Research report

**Objective:** Who was Raúl Alfonsín's first Minister of Defense?

**Status:** finished by the agent on turn 4

## Final result

Raúl Alfonsín's first Minister of Defense was Raúl Borrás. He served in this position from December 10, 1983, to May 25, 1985. This information is supported by discovery [1].

## Discoveries (1)

### 1. Turn 3, from https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ra%C3%BAl_Borr%C3%A1s

Raúl Borrás served as the Minister of Defense during the first years of Raúl Alfonsín's presidency, from December 10, 1983, to May 25, 1985.
