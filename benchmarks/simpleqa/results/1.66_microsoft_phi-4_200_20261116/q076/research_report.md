# Research report

**Objective:** The Dante-Alighieri monument, located on Allée des Poètes along Rue D'Auteuil, was created by L'atelier Attitude and inspired by the work of what Italian-born sculptor?

**Status:** finished by the agent on turn 4

## Final result

The Dante-Alighieri monument, located on Allée des Poètes along Rue D'Auteuil, was created by L'atelier Attitude and inspired by the work of the Italian-born sculptor Carlo Balboni. This information is supported by discovery [1].

## Discoveries (1)

### 1. Turn 3, from https://capverslesparcs.ccnq.org/commemorations/monument-dante-alighieri/

Le monument Dante-Alighieri est situé sur l’allée des Poètes, longeant la rue D’Auteuil. La sculpture est l’œuvre de L’atelier Attitude de Montréal et est inspirée du buste de Dante réalisé par Carlo Balboni.
