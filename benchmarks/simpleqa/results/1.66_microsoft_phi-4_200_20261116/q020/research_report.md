# Research report

**Objective:** In which month and year did Apple add the ability for users to speak "Hey Siri" to enable the assistant without the requirement of physically handling the device?

**Status:** finished by the agent on turn 4

## Final result

Apple added the ability for users to speak 'Hey Siri' to enable the assistant without the requirement of physically handling the device in October 2026. This feature was announced on October 13, 2026, as part of the new fourth-generation Apple TV 4K, which includes three built-in far-field microphones for hands-free Siri activation. [1]

## Discoveries (1)

### 1. Turn 3, from https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/13/new-apple-tv-4k-adds-far-field-microphones/

(Under the heading "New Apple TV 4K Adds Far-Field Microphones for Hands-Free Siri") Apple's new fourth-generation Apple TV 4K includes three built-in far-field microphones, allowing users to find something to watch and control playback without picking up the Siri Remote. Users can activate the voice assistant by saying 'Hey Siri' or 'Siri,' including while a movie or TV show is playing. The feature was announced on October 13, 2026.
