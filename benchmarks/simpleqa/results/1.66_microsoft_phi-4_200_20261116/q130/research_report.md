**Objective:** Who is credited with the binomial name of the yellowfin sole?

**Status:** finished by the agent on turn 4

## Final result

The binomial name of the yellowfin sole is credited to Pallas, who originally described it in 1814. This information is supported by discovery [1].

## Discoveries (1)

### 1. Turn 3, from https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellowfin_sole

The binomial name of the yellowfin sole is Limanda aspera, originally described by Pallas in 1814.
