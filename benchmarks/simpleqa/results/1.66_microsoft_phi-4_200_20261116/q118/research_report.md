## Final result

The Toronto mayors Sir Adam Wilson (Mayor from 1859-1861), John George Bowes (Mayor from 1861-1864), and Francis Henry Medcalf (Mayor from 1864-1867) were elected by different bodies during their terms. Sir Adam Wilson was elected by the public after the first official mayoral-candidate nomination meeting in 1858, as per the Municipal Act that allowed public voting for mayor [2]. John George Bowes, who succeeded Wilson, was also elected by the public following this new system [2]. Francis Henry Medcalf was initially elected by the public for three consecutive terms [2]; however, after the Municipal Act revisions in 1866, the selection of the mayor was handed back to the city council, and Medcalf was ousted by a council vote in January 1867 [2].
