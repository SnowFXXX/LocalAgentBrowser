# Research report

**Objective:** The Partisan weapon in the original version of Bloodstained: Ritual of the Night for the PC is dropped by which enemy with the word "armor" in its name?

**Status:** finished by the agent on turn 7

## Final result

The Partisan weapon in the original version of Bloodstained: Ritual of the Night for the PC is dropped by the enemy named Lance Armor. This information is supported by discovery [1].

## Discoveries (1)

### 1. Turn 5, from https://bloodstainedritualofthenight.wiki.fextralife.com/Partisan

Partisan is dropped by Lance Armor.
