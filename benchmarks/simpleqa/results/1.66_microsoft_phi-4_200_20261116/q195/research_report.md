# Research report

**Objective:** In which year did the United States Defense Health Agency first approve payment for sex reassignment surgery for an active-duty U.S. military service member?

**Status:** finished by the agent on turn 4

## Final result

The United States Defense Health Agency first approved payment for sex reassignment surgery for an active-duty U.S. military service member in 2017. An active-duty service member underwent gender transition surgery in 2017, which was the first such procedure approved under a waiver allowing the Pentagon to pay for the operation. Vice Admiral Raquel Bono approved the waiver request on November 13, 2017. [1]
