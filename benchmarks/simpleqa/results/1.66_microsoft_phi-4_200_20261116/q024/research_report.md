# Research report

**Objective:** What was the name of Project Zero Dawn's Extinction Failsafe Protocol in the video game Horizon Zero Dawn (2017)?

**Status:** finished by the agent on turn 4

## Final result

The name of Project Zero Dawn's Extinction Failsafe Protocol in the video game Horizon Zero Dawn (2017) is 'HADES.' HADES is a rogue artificial intelligence (AI) that was formerly a subordinate function of the AI GAIA, serving as the 'extinction failsafe protocol' for Project Zero Dawn. This information is supported by discovery [1].
