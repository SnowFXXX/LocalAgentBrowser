# Research report

**Objective:** What is the name of the sole survivor of the 2008 Yeti Airlines Flight 103 crash?

**Status:** finished by the agent on turn 11

## Final result

The name of the sole survivor of the 2008 Yeti Airlines Flight 103 crash is Surendra Kunwar, who was the captain of the flight. This information is supported by discoveries [1] and [2].

### 2. Turn 10, from http://www.airsafe.com/events/survivor.htm

(Under the heading "Plane crashes with a sole survivor") 8 October 2008; Yeti Airlines; DHC-6 Twin Otter; 9N-AFE; flight 103; Lukla, Nepal: The aircraft was on a domestic flight from Kathmandu to Lukla, Nepal. During the landing, the aircraft struck some rocks, crashed, and caught fire. Two of the three crew members and all 16 passengers were killed. The sole survivor was the captain, Surendra Kunwar.
