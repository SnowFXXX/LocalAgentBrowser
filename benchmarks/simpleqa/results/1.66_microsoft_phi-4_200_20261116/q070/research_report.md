# Research report

**Objective:** During which year did Gerhard Richter have a solo exhibition named "Gerhard Richter 4900 Colours: Version II"?

**Status:** finished by the agent on turn 4

## Final result

Gerhard Richter had a solo exhibition named 'Gerhard Richter 4900 Colours: Version II' in the year 2008. This exhibition was held at the Serpentine Gallery from 23 September to 23 November 2008. [1]
