# Research report

**Objective:** Which patch for the original Dark Souls reduced the effectiveness of the Hornet Ring?

**Status:** finished by the agent on turn 13

## Final result

The patch for the original Dark Souls that reduced the effectiveness of the Hornet Ring is Version 1.06, released on 22nd October 2012. This patch nerfed the Hornet Ring to provide a 30% critical bonus. This information is supported by discovery [2].
