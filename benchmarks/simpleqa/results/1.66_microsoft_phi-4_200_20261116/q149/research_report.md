# Research report

**Objective:** What total number of newly discovered pieces of music by Maddalena Casulana were played for the first time in 400 years on March 8, 2022, as part of BBC Radio 3's programming for International Women's Day?

**Status:** finished by the agent on turn 9

## Final result

The total number of newly discovered pieces of music by Maddalena Casulana that were played for the first time in 400 years on March 8, 2022, as part of BBC Radio 3's programming for International Women's Day is 12. This information is supported by discovery [2], which states that 17 madrigals have been added to her repertoire, and 12 of those will be premiered on that date.
