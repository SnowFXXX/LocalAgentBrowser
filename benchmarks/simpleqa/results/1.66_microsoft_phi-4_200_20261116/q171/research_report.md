# Research report

**Objective:** Which state of India was awarded the international honor for afforestation efforts at COP-28?

**Status:** finished by the agent on turn 4

## Final result

The state of India that was awarded the international honor for afforestation efforts at COP-28 is Bihar. This recognition was given due to the actions undertaken by the Bihar government in the field of afforestation, particularly through the Jal-Jeevan-Hariyali Abhiyan (Rural Development Department). These efforts received praise from the international community at the United Nations Climate Change Conference of the Parties (COP-28) in Dubai. [1]
