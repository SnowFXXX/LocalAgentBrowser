**Objective:** What Toronto Library collection was established in 1977, named after the children's librarian and head of the TPL?

**Status:** finished by the agent on turn 6

## Final result

The Toronto Library collection established in 1977 is the Jean Thomson Collection of Original Art. It was named after a distinguished children's librarian and head of the Public Service at Toronto Public Library. This information is supported by discovery [1].
