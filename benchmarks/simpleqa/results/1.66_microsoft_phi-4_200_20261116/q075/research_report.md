# Research report

**Objective:** Who originally recorded the scientific name of the white-headed duck as *Anas leucocephala* in 1769?

**Status:** finished by the agent on turn 4

## Final result

The scientific name of the white-headed duck as *Anas leucocephala* in 1769 was originally recorded by Giovani Antonio Scopoli. This information is supported by discovery [1].

## Discoveries (1)

### 1. Turn 3, from https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White-headed_duck

(Under the heading "Taxonomy and systematicsedit") The white-headed duck was originally described as Anas leucocephala by Giovani Antonio Scopoli in 1769.
