# Research report

**Objective:** On what day, month, and year did King Louis II's, also known as King Louis XIII of France, reign as King of Navarre end?

**Status:** finished by the agent on turn 4

## Final result

King Louis II, also known as King Louis XIII of France, reigned as King of Navarre until 20 October 1620, when the crown of Navarre was merged with the French crown. [1]
