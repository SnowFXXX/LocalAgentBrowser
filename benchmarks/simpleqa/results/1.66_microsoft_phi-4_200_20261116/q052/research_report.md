# Research report

**Objective:** Who is the municipality of Gómez Plata, Antioquia, Colombia, named after?

**Status:** finished by the agent on turn 7

## Final result

The municipality of Gómez Plata, Antioquia, Colombia, is named in memory of Juan de la Cruz Gómez Plata, an obispo neogranadino (bishop) of Antioquia. This information is supported by discovery [1].
