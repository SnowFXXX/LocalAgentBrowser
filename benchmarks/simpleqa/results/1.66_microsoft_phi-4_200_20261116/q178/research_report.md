# Research report

**Objective:** Name the first Indian artist to travel abroad for the purpose of studying printmaking as an art.

**Status:** finished by the agent on turn 4

## Final result

The first Indian artist to travel abroad for the purpose of studying printmaking as an art was Mukul Dey. While in Japan in 1916, Mukul Dey studied under Yokoyama Taikan and Kanzan Shimomura at Tokyo and Yokohama. [1]
