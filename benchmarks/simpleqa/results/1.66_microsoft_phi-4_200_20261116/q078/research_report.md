## Final result

Richard Serra created his work 'Thirty-Five Feet of Lead Rolled Up' while living in New York City. This is supported by the discovery that Serra moved to New York City in 1966 and created 'Thirty-Five Feet of Lead Rolled Up' in 1968 [3, 4].
