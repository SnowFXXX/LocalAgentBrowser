# Research report

**Objective:** What was the original name of the town of Dolores, Abra, Philippines?

**Status:** finished by the agent on turn 4

## Final result

The original name of the town of Dolores, Abra, Philippines was Bucao. This name was given in honor of the first Tingguian (Itneg) chieftain who settled in the place long before Spanish colonization. The town was renamed Dolores in 1885 in honor of the town's patron saint, Our Lady of Sorrows (Nuestra Señora de los Dolores). [1]
