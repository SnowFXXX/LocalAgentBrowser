# Research report

**Objective:** On which date, month, and year was the "King Shaka International Airport" name approved by the South African Geographical Names Council?

**Status:** finished by the agent on turn 9

## Final result

The name 'King Shaka International Airport' was approved by the South African Geographical Names Council on 14 January 2010. This information is supported by discovery [1].

## Discoveries (1)

### 1. Turn 8, from https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Shaka_International_Airport

(Under the heading "Naming processedit") The airport name was approved by the South African Geographical Names Council on 14 January 2010.
