## Final result

The species of Polymixia fish named after research scientist Gloria Hollister is Polymixia hollisterae, Grande & Wilson, 2021. This species is named in honor of Gloria E. Hollister (Anable), B.S., M.S. (1900–1988), who was a pioneering ichthyologist, a key member of the William Beebe bathysphere expeditions in Bermuda, a world record holder for deep-sea descent by a woman, a leader of tropical zoological expeditions, a pioneer of the Red Cross Blood Bank, and a ground-breaking conservationist.
