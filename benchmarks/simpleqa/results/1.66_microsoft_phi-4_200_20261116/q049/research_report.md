# Research report

**Objective:** Other than being a choreographer for the TV series In Living Color, what other job did Rosie Perez do on the show?

**Status:** finished by the agent on turn 6

## Final result

Other than being a choreographer for the TV series In Living Color, Rosie Perez also worked as a segment producer on the show. This information is supported by discovery [2].
