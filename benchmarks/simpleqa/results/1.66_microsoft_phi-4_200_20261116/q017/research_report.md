# Research report

**Objective:** What's the full name of the first female Finance Minister in Zambia?

**Status:** finished by the agent on turn 4

## Final result

The full name of the first female Finance Minister in Zambia is Edith Zewelani Nawakwi. This information is supported by the discovery [1], which states that Edith Zewelani Nawakwi was the first woman in Zambia to hold the post of Minister of Finance following her appointment in 1998.

## Discoveries (1)

### 1. Turn 3, from https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edith_Nawakwi

Edith Zewelani Nawakwi (24 June 1959 – 7 April 2025) was a Zambian politician and economist. She was the first woman in Zambia to hold the post of Minister of Finance following her appointment in 1998 since Zambia's independence 33 years previously to that time.
