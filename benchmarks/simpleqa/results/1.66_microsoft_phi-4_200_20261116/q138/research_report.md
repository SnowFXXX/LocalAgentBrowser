## Final result

The winner of the Godman-Salvin Medal in 2010 was Ian Newton. This information is supported by discovery [1].

## Discoveries (1)

### 1. Turn 3, from https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Godman-Salvin_Medal

The Godman-Salvin Medal is a medal of the British Ornithologists' Union awarded 'to an individual as a signal honour for distinguished ornithological work.' The winner in 2010 was Ian Newton.
