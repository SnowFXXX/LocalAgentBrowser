# Research report

**Objective:** Ivan Lyakhov located the Lyakhovsky Islands by following the tracks of which animal?

**Status:** finished by the agent on turn 6

## Final result

Ivan Lyakhov located the Lyakhovsky Islands by following the tracks of mammoths. This is supported by his theory that the islands were mainly formed by a substratum of bones and tusks of mammoths, implying he followed mammoth tracks ([1], [2]).

### 1. Turn 3, from https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivan_Lyakhov

(Under the heading "Expeditionedit") Lyakhov's intentions were mainly commercial, for he hoped to find mammoth ivory. His theory was that both the islands he explored, and which were later named after him Lyakhov Islands, and those he sighted in the distance but was not able to explore, were mainly formed by a substratum of bones and tusks of mammoths.
