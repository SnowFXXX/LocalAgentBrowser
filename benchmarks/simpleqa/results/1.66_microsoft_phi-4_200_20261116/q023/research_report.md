# Research report

**Objective:** How many people were involved in making the largest human DNA helix, achieved by the Medical University of Varna (Bulgaria) in Varna, Bulgaria, on April 23, 2016?

**Status:** finished by the agent on turn 4

## Final result

The largest human DNA helix, achieved by the Medical University of Varna (Bulgaria) in Varna, Bulgaria, on April 23, 2016, involved 4,000 participants. This information is supported by discovery [1].
