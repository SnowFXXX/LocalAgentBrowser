# Research report

**Objective:** Which patch for the original Dark Souls reduced the effectiveness of the Hornet Ring?

**Status:** finished by the agent on turn 8

## Final result

The patch that reduced the effectiveness of the Hornet Ring in the original Dark Souls was patch 1.06. Prior to this patch, the Hornet Ring boosted critical damage by 50%, but this was reduced to 30% with the implementation of patch 1.06. [1]
