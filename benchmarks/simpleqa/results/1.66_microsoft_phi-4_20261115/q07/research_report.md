# Research report

**Objective:** What two football teams were mentioned in the libel case against "The Saturday Evening Post" in 1963?

**Status:** finished by the agent on turn 5

## Final result

The two football teams mentioned in the libel case against 'The Saturday Evening Post' in 1963 were the Georgia Bulldogs and the Alabama Crimson Tide. The lawsuit involved allegations that Wally Butts, the former Georgia Bulldogs football coach, and Bear Bryant, the Alabama head coach, had conspired to fix games, specifically the 1962 season opener between the Bulldogs and the Crimson Tide, which Alabama won 35-0. ### 1. Turn 3, from https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curtis_Publishing_Co._v._Butts

(Under the heading "Backgroundedit") The case involved a libel lawsuit filed by the former Georgia Bulldogs football coach Wally Butts against The Saturday Evening Post. The lawsuit arose from an article in the magazine, which alleged that Butts, still Georgia's athletic director following his resignation as coach after the 1960 season, and the Alabama head coach Bear Bryant had conspired to fix games, specifically the 1962 season opener between the Bulldogs and Crimson Tide, which Alabama won 35-0.
