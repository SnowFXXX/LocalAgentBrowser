# Research report

**Objective:** What year was the Briggs & Stratton Raptor III engine released?

**Status:** finished by the agent on turn 4

## Final result

The Briggs & Stratton Raptor III engine was released in 1995. This information is supported by the discovery: "Raptor III: The final Raptor variant, released in 1995, had five horsepower and added a cast-alloy crank with a carbon-infused piston." [1]
