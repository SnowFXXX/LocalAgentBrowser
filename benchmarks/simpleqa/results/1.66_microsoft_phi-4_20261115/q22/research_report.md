# Research report

**Objective:** What is the name of Nobita Nobi's paternal grandfather?

**Status:** finished by the agent on turn 4

## Final result

The name of Nobita Nobi's paternal grandfather is Nobiru Nobi. This information is supported by discovery [1], which states that 'Nobiru Nobi is Nobita's paternal grandfather.'

## Discoveries (1)

### 1. Turn 3, from https://doraemon.fandom.com/wiki/Nobiru_Nobi

(Under the heading "Skin color") Nobiru Nobi is Nobita's paternal grandfather, the father-in-law of Tamako Nobi, the husband of Nobita's grandmother, and also the father of Nobirou Nobi and Nobisuke Nobi. When Nobita's father was little, he used to be treated in a disciplined manner by Nobita's grandfather. Nobita's paternal grandfather passed away before Nobita was born (or Nobisuke had married with Tamako). Nobita and Doraemon meet him by going to the past using a time machine. He appears to love his son a lot, but does not seem to express his affection to his son, so that he doesn't get spoiled. His physical appearance is almost identical to Nobisuke, except Nobiru has a mustache and a different hairstyle. He was shown to be very strict and rude toward his son, Nobisuke, because he valued physical strength and athleticism which Nobisuke lacked, but deep down he loved him so much as seen in the one episode when Nobisuke gets first prize in school in painting. Though Nobiru was being harsh towards him, he secretly took his painting (which Nobisuke has thrown in the dustbin) and preserved it, showing his love and affection towards his son.
