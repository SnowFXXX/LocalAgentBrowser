# Research report

**Objective:** From which dealer's shop did the Florentine authorities buy the "Pitti Tondo" in 1823?

**Status:** finished by the agent on turn 4

## Final result

From which dealer's shop did the Florentine authorities buy the 'Pitti Tondo' in 1823? The Florentine authorities bought the 'Pitti Tondo' in 1823 from the shop of the dealer Fedele Acciai. This information is supported by discovery [1].

## Discoveries (1)

### 1. Turn 3, from https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pitti_Tondo

(Under the heading "Historyedit") In 1823, the tondo was bought by the Florentine authorities for 200 scudi, from the shop of the dealer Fedele Acciai.
