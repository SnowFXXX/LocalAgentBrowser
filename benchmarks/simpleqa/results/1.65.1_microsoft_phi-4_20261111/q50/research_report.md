# Research report

**Objective:** How much (in USD) was Frida's self-portrait with loose hair sold for in an auction by Christie's, New York, in May of 1991?

**Status:** finished by the agent on turn 4

## Final result

Frida Kahlo's 'Self-Portrait with Loose Hair' was sold for a record $1.65 million at Christie's New York in May 1991. This information is supported by discovery [1].
