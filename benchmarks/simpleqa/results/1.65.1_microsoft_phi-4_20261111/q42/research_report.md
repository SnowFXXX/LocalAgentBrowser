**Objective:** What year was the municipality of San Andrés de Cuerquia, Antioquia, Colombia, founded?

**Status:** finished by the agent on turn 9

## Final result

The municipality of San Andrés de Cuerquia, Antioquia, Colombia, was founded in the year 1582. This is based on the discovery that the governor of the province of Popayán adjudged these lands to the Spanish officer Don Francisco Lopez de Rúa in 1582, who founded a town in the Guarcama Valley and named it San Andrés de Cauca [3].
