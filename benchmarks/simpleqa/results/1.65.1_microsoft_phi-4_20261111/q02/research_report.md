# Research report

**Objective:** How many original artworks were shown in the Society of Illustrators' first "Annual Exhibition"?

**Status:** finished by the agent on turn 8

## Final result

The Society of Illustrators' first 'Annual Exhibition' showed 350 original artworks. This information is supported by the discovery [1].

## Discoveries (1)

### 1. Turn 7, from https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_of_Illustrators

(Under the heading "Post-World War IIedit") In 1959, the society held its first Annual Exhibition, juried by Bob Peak, Bradbury Thompson, Stevan Dohanos, and others. It opened with 350 original works of art and led to the publication of the first Illustrators Annual.
