# Research report

**Objective:** Which president had a meeting with Kwame Nkrumah right before the Kulungugu bomb attack?

**Status:** finished by the agent on turn 4

## Final result

The president who had a meeting with Kwame Nkrumah right before the Kulungugu bomb attack was President Maurice Yaméogo of Upper Volta (now Burkina Faso). This meeting was to sign documents relating to the construction of the major hydroelectric project on the Volta which would become Lake Volta. [1]
