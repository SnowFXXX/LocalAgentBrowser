# Research report

**Objective:** How many children did Welsh journalist Sir Trevor Maldwyn Evans have with his wife, Margaret?

**Status:** finished by the agent on turn 6

## Final result

Welsh journalist Sir Trevor Maldwyn Evans had two children with his wife, Margaret. Their daughter was Marilyn Evans (later Butler, 1927–2014), and they had a son. This information is supported by discovery [1].
