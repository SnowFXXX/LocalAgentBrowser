**Objective:** In what month and year was the Azad Kashmir Plebiscite Front formed in Muzaffarabad?

**Status:** finished by the agent on turn 4

## Final result

The Azad Kashmir Plebiscite Front was formed in Muzaffarabad in April 1965. This information is supported by discovery [1].

## Discoveries (1)

### 1. Turn 3, from https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azad_Kashmir_Plebiscite_Front

The Plebiscite Front in Azad Kashmir, also called Mahaz-i-Raishumari, was founded by Amanullah Khan in collaboration with Abdul Khaliq Ansari and Maqbool Bhat in April 1965 in Muzaffarabad.
