# Code of Conduct

This project aims to be a welcoming place for anyone interested in local AI agents, whatever their
experience, background or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and first-time contributors.
- Assume good intent, and keep criticism focused on code and ideas, not people.
- Give credit for other people's work and ideas.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and be willing to change your mind.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and commits) and when representing the
project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, please report it privately to the maintainer,
[@SnowFXXX](https://github.com/SnowFXXX), or use GitHub's
[reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
Reports will be handled promptly and in confidence. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments,
commits, code and issues that do not follow this code of conduct, and may temporarily or permanently block
anyone whose behaviour is harmful.

This code of conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
