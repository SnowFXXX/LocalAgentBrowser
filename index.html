Local Agent Browser

Ask a question. Get an answer with its sources.

LocalAgentBrowser searches the web, reads the pages and writes you an answer that shows where every part came from. It runs on your own PC, free and private.

Get LM Studio

The free app that runs the AI model on your PC: lmstudio.ai.

STEP 3

Open LocalAgentBrowser

From your desktop or the Start menu.

STEP 4

Ask

Type your question, click Start research, and watch it work.
