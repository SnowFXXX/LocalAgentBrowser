### How to Make Cold Brew Coffee at Home

#### What You Need with Quantities
- 1 cup (about 100g) coarsely ground coffee [2]
- 4 cups (about 1 litre) cold, filtered water [2]
- A 1.5L or 2L mason jar with lid [2]
- A fine-mesh sieve [2]
- A paper coffee filter, cheesecloth, or paper towel [2]
- A second clean jar or pitcher [2]

#### Steps in Order
1. Add the grounds to the jar and tap it on the counter to settle them flat.
2. Pour in half the water slowly; watch it bloom as the grounds swell and release CO2, wait 30 seconds.
3. Stir gently using a long spoon to ensure every ground is wet.
4. Add the remaining water and stir once more.
5. Seal the lid and refrigerate for 16 hours or overnight [2].
6. Strain through a fine-mesh sieve into your second jar.
7. Filter again through a paper filter, cheesecloth, or paper towel to remove fine sediment [2].
8. Discard the spent grounds (or save them for compost) [2]
9. Seal and refrigerate; your cold brew is ready to drink [2]

#### How Long It Keeps in the Fridge
Cold brew lasts 7 to 14 days in the fridge when stored in an airtight container [3].

### 2. Turn 6, from https://brewingcoffees.com/how-to-make-cold-brew-coffee/

You will need
- 1 cup (about 100g) coarsely ground coffee
- 4 cups (about 1 litre) cold, filtered water
A 1.5L or 2L mason jar with lid
A fine-mesh sieve
A paper coffee filter, cheesecloth, or paper towel
A second clean jar or pitcher
Method
Add the grounds to the jar. Tap it on the counter to settle them flat.
Pour in half the water slowly. Watch it bloom, the grounds will swell and release CO2. Wait 30 seconds.
Stir gently. Use a long spoon to make sure every ground is wet. Dry pockets will not extract.
Add the remaining water. Stir once more, then seal the lid.
Refrigerate for 16 hours. Set a timer or leave it overnight. 12 hours is the minimum, 24 hours is the maximum before bitterness starts to creep in.
Strain through a fine-mesh sieve into your second jar. This removes the bulk of the grounds.
Filter again through a paper filter or cheesecloth. This removes the fine sediment that makes cold brew gritty. Do not skip this step, even if it takes 10 minutes to drip through.
Discard the spent grounds (or save them for the garden, they are excellent compost).
Seal and refrigerate. Your cold brew is ready to drink. See the storage section below for how long it lasts.

### 3. Turn 9, from https://brewingcoffees.com/how-to-make-cold-brew-coffee/

(Under the heading "How long does cold brew last?") Cold brew lasts 7 to 14 days in the fridge when stored in an airtight container.
