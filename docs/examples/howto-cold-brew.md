(Under the heading "How To Make Cold Brew Coffee") Making cold brew at home is easy and requires just coffee beans, water, and a strainer. This method creates smooth, less-bitter coffee that's perfect for chilled drinks. Serve it over ice, add milk, or heat it for a hot cup—customize it to suit your mood.

(Under the heading "Why Make This") Steps for Making Cold Brew Coffee:
1. Grind the coffee coarsely.
2. Combine the grounds with water.
3. Let it steep overnight, or for around 12 hours.
4. Strain the coffee the next morning.

You can adjust the concentration of your cold brew coffee by changing the ratio of beans to water. Start with one cup of beans steeped in four cups of water. This will make a fairly concentrated coffee on its own, but it's perfect for pouring over ice or mixing with milk - or both. If that ratio of beans to water isn't quite to your taste, adjust it up or down until you hit the perfect balance for you.
